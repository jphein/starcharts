Stars in Two Hands

A look at the curated SVG presets, and the LLM-generated SVG customs they sit beside.

Curated presets

Hand-designed SVG. Each preset has its own palette, glow, and small animation. Stars within a preset are identical — beauty in clusters comes from arrangement, not per-star variance.

LLM-generated customs

User types a short prompt. An LLM (Claude, GPT-4) writes a one-shot SVG. The output renders natively in the sky — vector, transparent, animatable, free to scale. These three are written by hand to demonstrate what the LLM should aim for.

Firefly Star

a star made of fireflies and starlight

Woven Star

a star woven from wheat and gold thread

Dragon's Eye

a dragon's eye, watching, made of stars

Each preset SVG is portable as a React component — drop the <svg> into JSX, parameterize size via a prop, and you have your renderer.

Generated stars follow the same shape: the LLM returns SVG inside a 120 × 120 viewBox with the star centered at (0, 0), transparent background, and any animations expressed as inline CSS keyframes or class hooks.
